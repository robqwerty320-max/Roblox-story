# "Trust Me Bro" — meme short (approved by the owner)

- Format: vertical 1080x1920, 30 fps (final), ~40 s, English audience.
- Style: Roblox-*style* blocky characters (our own designs — no Roblox logo, no official avatars/faces, no "oof" sound),
  exaggerated cartoon faces swapped per beat, in the 3D toon world of this repo (ink lines, lens blur, gliding camera).
- Audio: no voice yet (no TTS available) → word-by-word English captions carry the narration, plus synthesized music + SFX.
  Keep `NARRATION` timings so a voice track can be dropped in later.

## Characters
- **Gary the Noob** — blocky, naive, bead eyes, mouth always slightly open. Orange head, purple torso (own palette).
- **ScamLord_99** — scammer in sunglasses and a gold chain, tooth that sparkles ("ding").
- **Tiny_Timmy** — small kid player: huge head, tiny body, innocent giant eyes.

## Face library (sprite swaps on the head's front face)
hypno-spirals, dollar-sign eyes, tear fountains, sliding sunglasses, empty "acceptance" stare, Timmy's innocent eyes,
pinpoint pupils, jaw-to-the-floor, twitching eye, giant sweat drop.
