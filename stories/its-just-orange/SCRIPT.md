# "It's Just Orange" — 2D meme short (~20 s)

- Format: vertical 1080x1920, **2D** (no three.js world): flat cartoon drawing in SVG/Canvas inside Remotion.
  Render at 30 fps; character drawings change **on twos** (new pose every 2 frames = 15 drawings/s), hits, smears and
  impact frames **on ones**. Camera moves are smooth every frame.
- Pace reference: animated meme-dialogue shorts (the kind discussed in the "3D анимация персонажей" chat):
  ~20 s, 7 lines, a new shot/angle every ~1.4 s on average, nothing static, lip flaps on every line.
- Characters: Roblox-*style* blocky people of our own design (box head, box torso, stick-ish box limbs, bold black
  outline, flat colours + one cel-shadow tone). No Roblox logo, no official avatar faces, no "oof".
- **Sound: NO music. SFX only, and no musical instruments** (no drums, stings, choirs, strings, piano). Allowed:
  whooshes, footsteps, sizzle, poof, bonk, splat, cloth flap, crowd gasp, cartoon spring "boing", record scratch,
  bubbling lava, character gibberish voice blips (synthesised chirps per syllable), wind, silence.
- Text: speech bubbles (or word-by-word captions) in English, timed to the lip flaps. No voice actor yet.

## Characters
- **Noob** — orange head, purple torso, teal legs (own palette): huge blank eyes, mouth always slightly open, overconfident.
- **Pro** — black hoodie, red visor, tired eyes; the straight man.
- **Dev** — tiny kid with a giant head and a "DEV" name tag; appears from nowhere.

## Animation rules
- Every shot moves: push-ins, whip pans with blur streaks, crash zooms, shakes on impacts (flip sign each frame, decay 7 f).
- Anticipation → action → overshoot on every big move; squash & stretch on jumps and landings.
- Exaggerated faces swapped per beat: blank confident, shocked pinpoint pupils, inflated yelling head, charcoal deadpan,
  smug innocent dev, facepalm.
- Lip flaps: open/closed/wide mouth shapes driven by the syllables of each line; gibberish voice blips on the same syllables.
